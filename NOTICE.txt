Ozone and child asthma emergency visits, New York City, 2016-2025
==================================================================

Not held here: the daily counts. They are fetched from the authors' Zenodo
record when the example is opened, and checked by digest.

Source:   Lian D, et al. Data and code for: Ambient ozone and pediatric asthma
          emergency department visits in New York City. Version 1.0. Zenodo.
          doi:10.5281/zenodo.22757330
          The authors license their own materials under CC BY 4.0, and state
          that the underlying observations "remain subject to each custodian's
          applicable terms".

Custodian of the counts: New York City Department of Health and Mental
          Hygiene, EpiQuery. NYC.gov's terms reserve all rights. This
          repository does not redistribute the counts.

Held here: the outlines of New York City's five boroughs, from geoBoundaries
          USA ADM2 (release 9469f09), drawn by the US Census Bureau,
          MAF/TIGER Database. Public domain.

Not held here: the ozone monitors' readings and positions. They are read
          from the same Zenodo package; the package names US EPA AQS as
          their custodian.

The authors and the custodians are not associated with this example and have
not endorsed it.
